# Save-derived research multiplier tree

The research simulator multiplies observation EXP by the account bonuses a
decoded save carries. `crates/account/src/` is the port of upstream
`js/save/context.js` (the context) and `js/stats/defs/research-exp.js` (the
tree); `crates/research/src/sim.rs` ports `simTotalExpWith` and the insight and
kaleidoscope formulas from `js/stats/systems/w7/research-math.js`.

## How an import builds it

1. `save::import` decodes the research rows, then calls `account::account`.
2. `Decoder::context` reads every numeric term `simTotalExpWith` needs.
3. `Decoder::tree` walks the descriptor's additive items and true multipliers
   in upstream order and stores them in `Imported::tree`.
4. `SimContext::extra_pct` is the additive total minus the sticker, grid and
   mastery terms, because the simulator re-applies those itself; this is the
   upstream `cachedExtPctExSticker` definition.

`Imported::tree` is the breakdown: `additive` holds percentages,
`multipliers` holds factors, and `BonusTerm::omitted` names the save field a
term could not read. Every omission also appears in `Imported::warnings`.

## Omitted terms

Two upstream steps are left out and warned about, because they need systems
that are their own planned sections:

- **`SL_<char>`** — the Tome star talent slot needs the talent, guild, bribe
  and stamp systems. It is left at 0.
- **`PVStatList_<char>`** — the Tome score's live drop-rate refresh needs the
  combat stats. The saved `OptLacc[200]` value is used instead.

On the committed fixture the first omits 255 of the 50,912 Tome points and the
second changes no point totals; together they shift EXP/hr by `2e-5` relative.
`crates/import/tests/fixture.rs` pins every other term to its upstream value and
the rate to a `1e-4` relative tolerance.

## Dashboard AFK gains

The dashboard's AFK rate is the account-wide `research-afk-gains.js` descriptor:
`min(1, 0.01 + sum / 100)`, summed from companion 28, companion 153 (plus its
second level), the minehead floor bonuses 1 and 10, grid 71 and 111, sailing
artifact 36 (capped at 6), card `w7b11` (capped at 10), gem shop slot 45
(`2 × GemItemsPurchased[45]`) and Ring of Glory 4 and 24.

`SimContext` carries the account terms (`companion_28`,
`companion_153_level2`, `sailing_art36`, `card_w7b11`, `gem_shop_45`); the grid
and minehead terms come from the live simulation. Gambit Milestone 15 is listed
in the tree but left out of the sum, because its `gambitPTSmulti` chain (Hole
measurements, cosmo, bolaia, monument, jar, arcane and legend terms) is not
modelled yet. The term is reported as not modelled, never guessed.

## Run options

`idleon_research::model::SimOptions` carries the three upstream Optimizer toggles;
`engine::simulate` and the magnifier optimizer read them.

- **Assume observations unlocked** (`assumeObsUnlocked`) marks every observation
  whose `roll` requirement the new research level meets as found on each level-up,
  as `simForwardProjection` does. The rate sees them through the found-observation
  terms and through the multiplier that counts found observations.
- **Extended insight lookahead** (`extendInsightLA`) makes the monocle optimizer
  score a target by `permanent rate gain × insight/hour` (the upstream
  `chooseMonoTargets` candidate score) instead of the next level-up speed alone.
  The permanent gain is the EXP rate one more insight level buys, so it is zero
  for an observation with no magnifying glass unless the grid's total-insight
  bonus (node 94) applies. Upstream ships this toggle checked.
- **Tournament +12h** (`includeTournament`) adds the daily Research Registrant's
  12 hours of raw research time at each 24h boundary, plus one insight
  collection for the monocle board. The first boundary comes from
  `TimeAway.GlobalTime`, half an hour before the 20:00 UTC reset, and is skipped
  a day when `OptLacc[511]` shows the cached tournament day (`OptLacc[496]`)
  already registered. Without a save clock the fallback is 24 hours.

`Target::validate` rejects a level at or below the current research level and
hours below one; the Optimizer pane disables its button and names the problem
instead of running. The target and the three toggles live in the research
section config, so they survive tab switches, dashboard visits and the session
file.

## Target-aware run

`idleon_research::engine::simulate` is the port of upstream `unifiedSim`
(`js/sim-engine.js`). The rate is constant between events, so it jumps to the next research
level-up, insight level-up, tournament registration or the target's horizon, and handles the
level-up's side effects:

- every research level-up spends the grid points the new level earned with the marginal climb,
  re-places the shapes and re-fits the magnifiers;
- every insight level-up re-fits the magnifiers when the grid reads insight for EXP (nodes 93 and
  94, upstream `insightAffectsExp`). The shapes are re-placed on research level-ups only, because
  that is when the owned count, the level-90 rotation unlock and the grid spend change what a shape
  is worth; re-placing them again for an insight-only event would repeat a search that cannot move
  them;
- with *assume observations unlocked* every observation whose `roll` the new level meets is marked
  found, and the tournament toggle injects its 12h of raw research time at the boundary.

`Refit::Fixed` is the plain projection. `optimize::run` builds the optimized layout as before, then
runs the saved layout and the built one through the same loop: a level target keeps the layout that
reaches the level first, an hour target the one that earns the most EXP in the window, and a tie
keeps the optimized layout. `optimize::grid::spend_for_target` ranks the marginal climb against the
full grid search the same way, so a point is spent for the target it buys instead of its immediate
bonus.

The run has no random source, the candidates are visited in a fixed order and ties go to the first
candidate, so the same save and options reproduce it exactly. `advanceResearchLevel` lands on the
level curve with the upstream `1e-9` slack: an exact jump can undershoot the requirement by a
rounding error, and without it the run would spend its jumps crawling the residual.

## Verifying against upstream

Run the upstream loader on a save and print the context and tree:

```console
node -e "import('/path/to/upstream/js/save/loader.js').then(async m => {
  m.loadSaveData(JSON.parse(require('fs').readFileSync('fixtures/data.json','utf8')));
  const { buildSaveContext, makeSimCtx } = await import('/path/to/upstream/js/save/context.js');
  console.log(buildSaveContext(), makeSimCtx((await import('/path/to/upstream/js/state.js')).saveData.gridLevels, buildSaveContext()));
})"
```

The fixture's expected values in `crates/import/tests/fixture.rs` were produced
that way.
